# Sources and notices

## Publication metadata

Candidate works are identified through ORCID `0000-0002-8160-2446` and Europe PMC's exact `AUTHORID` query. DOI and bibliographic metadata may also be checked through Crossref. The public PWA does not contain or redistribute full abstracts.

## Pronunciation

British/RP IPA entries include data derived from English Wiktionary, which is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license. Technical terms and abbreviations are additionally curated for this project. Actual playback is produced by the user's device through the Web Speech API, preferably with an `en-GB` voice.

## Learning content

Chinese meanings and short examples were prepared for personal educational use in the context of ubiquitin biology, protease biology, mass spectrometry, proteomics, and scientific communication. Generated examples are not quotations from article abstracts.
